# Smart Fitness

**智能健身产品与移动应用设计**

A connected fitness concept spanning a physical product, a mobile application and visual identity.

把健身实体产品、移动端健康记录与品牌视觉整合为一套产品系统，覆盖运动、体重、饮食和社区等使用场景。

**Tools & technologies:** Figma · Product design · Mobile UI · 3D visualisation · Visual identity

## Design work
- Physical fitness-product concept and 3D visualisation.
- Mobile onboarding and activity/health dashboards.
- Weight and nutrition tracking, workout content and community participation.
- Product presentation and a coordinated visual language across physical and digital touchpoints.
