Making marine pollution tangible让海洋污染变得可感知

An interactive environmental education product for children aged 4–12. A physical marine scene, detectors and light feedback support an experience about pollution and conservation.

Prototype & evaluation原型与评估

The team report documents user testing at a wetland museum. Feedback called for clearer guidance for younger children and richer interaction and information for older children.
