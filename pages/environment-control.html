Sense, decide & actuate环境感知、判断与执行

A separate Arduino exercise combines LDR light readings with humidity measurements to control a DC motor. The motor starts when the environment is dark and humidity is below a threshold, and stops when either condition is not met.

Arduino sketch / Arduino 源码 →Project notes / 项目说明

System concept & wiring系统构想与接线

Physical implementation实物实现

The concept diagrams show Leonardo/DHT22, while the implementation screenshot shows Uno/DHT11. The archived sketch is another revision with different pins and thresholds. This local controller is separate from the ESP8266/MQTT networked project.
