# Lightsaber Jump Rope

**光剑跳绳：游戏化健身交互概念**

A cordless jump-rope concept that maps exercise rhythms to game actions.

将无绳跳绳动作映射为音乐跑酷中的挥剑、跳跃和攻击操作，通过即时反馈和多人积分机制提升参与感。

**Tools & technologies:** Interaction design · Motion-input mapping · Product modelling · Rendering

## Interaction concept
The cordless rope handles also act as motion controllers. The design maps one jump to a sword action, a rapid double jump to clearing an obstacle, and three jumps to a second jump action. Side buttons switch interaction modes.

A music/running-game scenario gives users feedback through obstacles, enemies, scores and a proposed household leaderboard.
